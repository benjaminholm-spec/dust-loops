DUST LOOPS - a tape-loop drone instrument and effect for Logic Pro
A free passion project by Dust Planet Records.

Needs: a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later; Intel Macs are not supported), macOS 14 Sonoma or newer,
and Logic Pro (or another app that loads Audio Unit plugins).


INSTALL (about a minute)

1. Double-click "Install Dust Loops.command".

2. macOS will probably refuse to open it the first time, because the plugin isn't registered with Apple
   (that costs $99 a year, and this is a free passion project). To allow it:
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     - Scroll down. Next to the message about "Install Dust Loops.command", click "Open Anyway".
     - Confirm with your password if asked, then click "Open".
   (On macOS 14 you can also right-click the installer, choose Open, then click Open.)

3. A Terminal window opens, installs both plugins, and says "Done".

4. Restart Logic Pro. You'll find it under:
     Instrument slot > AU Instruments > Dust Planet Records > Dust Loops
     Audio FX slot > Audio Units > Dust Planet Records > Dust Loops FX


UNINSTALL

Double-click "Uninstall Dust Loops.command" (allow it the same way as above).
